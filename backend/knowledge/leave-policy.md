# Leave Policy

This policy explains the paid leave available to permanent employees of Keystone Group and how to request it. Contractors should check their contract.

## Leave entitlement

Every permanent full-time employee receives, per calendar year:

- **Annual leave:** 20 working days
- **Sick leave:** 10 working days
- **Casual leave:** 6 working days

Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Employees who join during the year receive a pro-rated entitlement from their start date, rounded up to the nearest half day.

## Requesting annual leave

Request annual leave in the Keystone HR app or the HR portal under **Time off → New request**. Your manager is notified and should respond within 3 working days.

- Requests of up to 2 days should be made at least 5 working days in advance.
- Requests of 3 days or more should be made at least 4 weeks in advance.
- Leave longer than 10 consecutive working days needs approval from your manager and your department head.

You can cancel a pending or approved request at any time before the leave starts. The days return to your balance.

## Sick leave

If you are unwell, tell your manager before your normal start time on the first day of absence, and record the sick day in the HR app when you are able.

A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick leave can't be used in advance and can't be carried over.

## Casual leave

Casual leave covers short, unplanned personal needs such as a family matter or a home emergency. It can be taken in half days and requested on the same day. Casual leave can't be combined with annual leave to extend a holiday and can't be carried over.

## Carry-over and expiry

You may carry over up to **5 days** of unused annual leave into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**, after which they expire. Unused sick and casual leave always expires on 31 December.

Unused annual leave isn't paid out, except on leaving the company, where any remaining days for the current year are paid in the final salary.

## Public holidays

Keystone Group observes the public holidays of the country you work in. The list for each office is published in the HR portal every December. If you are required to work on a public holiday, you receive a replacement day off to be used within 60 days.
