# Code of Conduct

The Code of Conduct sets out how we work with each other, our clients and partners. Every employee confirms they have read it when they join and once a year after that.

## Respect and inclusion

We treat everyone with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated, whether in person, in writing or online, and whether at work or at work events.

## Conflicts of interest

Tell your manager and Legal and Compliance about any situation where your personal interests could conflict with the company's, such as a second job, an investment in a supplier, or a family member working for a client. Most conflicts can be managed once they are declared.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to **75 USD** from a client or supplier. Anything above that must be declared in the compliance portal and approved before you accept it. Cash or vouchers can never be accepted.

## Protecting information

Customer and employee data may only be used for the purpose it was collected for. Don't share confidential information outside the company without a signed agreement, and don't store it on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.

## Speaking up

If you see something that breaks this Code, raise it with your manager, the People Team or Legal and Compliance. You can also report anonymously through the **Speak Up** line, available 24 hours a day. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a serious breach of this Code.
