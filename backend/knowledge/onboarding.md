# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Keystone Group. This guide explains what happens in your first weeks and what you need to complete.

## Before your first day

You receive an email with your start time, office address and your manager's name. Upload your ID proof, address documents and bank details in the HR portal before your first day so that your first salary is paid on time.

## Your first week

On day one you collect your laptop from IT, set up multi-factor authentication and meet your manager. During the first week, you:

- Complete information security training
- Read and accept the Code of Conduct
- Meet your onboarding buddy, who answers everyday questions for your first 3 months
- Agree your 30-60-90 day goals with your manager

## Mandatory training

Information security, data protection and anti-harassment training must be completed within **14 days** of your start date. The courses are in the learning portal and take about 3 hours in total. Overdue training is reported to your manager.

## Probation

Probation lasts **6 months**. You have a check-in with your manager at 3 months and a review at the end of probation. The notice period during probation is 2 weeks.

## Getting help

For IT problems, contact the IT service desk through the HR app or the portal. For questions about pay, leave or benefits, raise an HR request in the HR app. Your onboarding buddy and your manager are always good first contacts.
