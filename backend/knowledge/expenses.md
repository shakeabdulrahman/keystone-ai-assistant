# Travel and Expenses Policy

This policy covers costs you pay personally while working for Keystone Group, and how to get reimbursed.

## What you can claim

You can claim reasonable costs incurred for business purposes: travel, accommodation, meals while travelling, client entertainment approved in advance, and work equipment approved by your manager. Personal costs such as fines, minibar items or upgrades for comfort can't be claimed.

## Limits

- **Meals while travelling:** up to 50 USD per day
- **Hotels:** up to 180 USD per night, or 250 USD in high-cost cities listed in the Finance portal
- **Flights:** economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy for longer flights
- **Taxis:** allowed when public transport isn't practical, or after 21:00

## How to submit a claim

Submit claims in the expenses app within **30 days** of the expense. Attach an itemised receipt for every item over 10 USD. Your manager approves the claim, and approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times a year can request a corporate card. Card spending follows the same limits and still needs receipts in the expenses app within 30 days.

## Late or missing receipts

Claims submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed. If a receipt is lost, submit a signed missing-receipt declaration; this is allowed at most twice a year.
