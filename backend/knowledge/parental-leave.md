# Parental Leave Policy

Keystone Group supports employees when they welcome a child through birth, adoption or surrogacy. This policy applies to all permanent employees from their first day of employment.

## Primary caregiver leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to **26 weeks of fully paid leave**. Leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected due date or placement date. At least 16 weeks must be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or placement.

## Secondary caregiver leave

The secondary caregiver is entitled to **6 weeks of fully paid leave**, which can be taken in up to three blocks of at least one week each within the first 12 months.

## Adoption and surrogacy

Employees who adopt or become parents through surrogacy have the same entitlement as birth parents. The leave starts from the date of placement. Up to 5 days of paid leave is also available for adoption appointments and legal proceedings.

## How to apply

Tell your manager and the People Team at least **8 weeks** before you plan to start your leave, and submit a **Parental leave** request in the HR portal. Include the expected date of birth or placement. A medical or official document is needed before the leave begins.

## Returning to work

On returning, you may work a phased return at 60% of your hours on full pay for up to 4 weeks. You are entitled to return to the same role, or a role of equal level and pay. Parents of children under 3 can request flexible working arrangements, which managers must consider within 2 weeks.

## Pay and benefits during leave

Your salary, health insurance, pension contributions and leave accrual continue as normal during parental leave. Bonuses are calculated as if you had been working.
