# Employee Benefits Guide

A summary of the benefits Keystone Group offers to permanent employees. Details may differ by country; the HR portal has the plan documents for your office.

## Health insurance

Health insurance covers you, your partner and your children from your first day. It includes hospital care, outpatient care, dental check-ups and vision care. You can add or change dependants within 30 days of a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child, or during the annual enrolment window in November.

## Pension

Keystone Group contributes **6% of your base salary** to the company pension plan. If you contribute at least 3%, the company adds another 2%. You can change your contribution once a month in the HR portal.

## Wellbeing

Every employee has a **wellbeing allowance of 500 USD per year** for gym memberships, sports classes, mental-health apps or therapy sessions. The Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential counselling 24 hours a day.

## Learning and development

Each employee has a learning budget of **1,000 USD per year** and 5 learning days for courses, certifications and conferences relevant to their role. Ask your manager to approve the course, then book it through the learning portal.

## Referral bonus

If you refer a candidate who is hired and stays for 6 months, you receive a referral bonus of 1,500 USD, paid with the following month's salary.
