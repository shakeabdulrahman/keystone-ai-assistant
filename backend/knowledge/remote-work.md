# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

Keystone Group works in a hybrid model. Teams agree how they work best, within the rules below.

## Hybrid working

Employees in office-based roles work from the office at least **2 days per week**. Teams choose a shared anchor day so that people meet in person. Your manager can agree a different pattern for a role that needs it.

## Working from home

When working from home you must be reachable during your team's core hours of **10:00 to 16:00** local time, use a private space for confidential calls, and connect through the company VPN.

## Working from another location

You may work from another location in your own country for up to 30 days per year. Working from another country is allowed for up to **20 working days per calendar year** and needs approval from your manager and the People Team at least 2 weeks in advance, because of tax and immigration rules.

## Home office equipment

New employees receive a laptop, monitor, keyboard and headset. A one-time **home office allowance of 300 USD** (or local equivalent) is available for a chair or desk; claim it through expenses with a receipt within your first 6 months.

## Security when working remotely

Never leave your laptop unlocked or unattended in public places. Don't use public Wi-Fi without the VPN. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk immediately, at any time of day.
